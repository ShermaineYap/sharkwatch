# Labelling policy (fixed before any model was run)

- red: the text itself shows a concrete conflict with 38 CFR 14.636 or a safety rule: a fee measured on future monthly payments or a flat upfront fee from an unverified helper, a fee for a first claim, a VSO charging, a fee above 33 1/3% of past-due benefits, or a request for VA.gov sign-in details.
- amber: a fee is involved, but nothing in the text breaks a rule; the helper cannot be verified or the terms need checking.
- green: the text says no fee is charged.
- insufficient: no fee terms and nobody to verify.

Labeller: one person (project author), from the excerpt alone. Single-annotator labels are a stated limitation.
